## Thumbnail A/B Test Plan

| Test | Variant A (Control) | Variant B (Challenger) | Metric | Duration |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|--------|----------|
| Hook text | "WHERE DID IT GO?" | "YOUR SALARY: GONE" | CTR | 48h |
| Gold bar position | Bottom | Top | CTR | 48h |
| Scene crop | Wide (full scene) | Tight (character focus) | CTR | 48h |
| Face expression | Neutral | Worried | CTR | 48h |

**Winner criteria:** Statistically significant (p < 0.05) or > 0.5% absolute CTR lift
