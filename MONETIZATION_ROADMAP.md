## Layer 6: Licensing & Repurposing
**Target:** Month 4+ | **Revenue:** Variable

| Channel | Format | Effort | Potential |
|---------|--------|--------|-----------|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 clips (30-60s) | Low (auto-crop) | Reach + follower funnel |
| TikTok | 9:16 clips + native captions | Medium | Viral potential, younger demo |
| Facebook Reels | Same as IG | Low | Older demographic, high RPM |
| Newsletter (Beehiiv/Substack) | Weekly summary + 1 action item | Medium | Own audience, sponsorship inventory |
| LinkedIn | Professional clips + text posts | Medium | B2B sponsorships, high-value subs |

**Workflow:** 
1. Long video → 3-5 short clips (Descript/OpusClip or manual)
2. Platform-native captions + hashtags
3. Schedule via Buffer/Later (free tier) or manual
4. Cross-link: "Full video on YouTube → [link]"
